About Waymark

Waymark is a rules-light roleplaying game written for people who have never played one, and for experienced players who want something quick to run. It is free to read, print, and share.

This is draft {{VERSION}}. The rules are still being tested and will change.

Where it comes from

Waymark is based on Cairn by Yochai Gal. Cairn supplies the foundation: three attributes, saves, Hit Protection, attacks that always hit, inventory slots, Fatigue, and Scars. Waymark adds Origins, Abilities and Affinities, Tiers, corruption, imbued gear, and tabletop movement, and rewrites the whole for newcomers.

A cairn is a pile of stones that marks a trail. A waymark is any sign that does the same job.

Waymark is an independent work. It is not connected with or approved by Cairn or its author.
