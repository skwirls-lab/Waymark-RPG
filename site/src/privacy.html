Privacy policy

Draft of 25 November 2026.

This page says what the Waymark app knows about you, why, who else handles it, and how to get it back or have it deleted. It is written to be read. If something is unclear, write to {{CONTACT}}.

Waymark is run by {{LEGAL_NAME}} (“we”). This policy covers the Waymark app at {{APP_HOST}}. The rules site at {{SITE_HOST}} has no accounts and stores nothing about you.

The short version

  • You can play as a guest and give us no name, no email, and no password.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, your email address and your Google profile picture address. We use them to show you your own account and to let you back in.
  • We store what you make: characters, campaigns, notes, and what happens in play.
  • We do not sell your information. We do not show adverts. We do not track you across other sites.
  • You can download what we hold about you, or delete your account and everything in it, from the Account page.

What we store

If you play as a guest

A random identifier kept in your browser, the name you type when you join a campaign, and whatever you make. If you clear your browser's storage, the guest account is gone and we cannot recover it.

If you sign in with Google

Your Google account's name, email address, and profile picture address, and an identifier for your account. We never see your Google password.

What you make and do

  • Characters: the sheet, name, description, and notes.
  • Campaigns you run: the name, the brief, the timeline, cards, your private notes, creatures and other content you write, and the fight in progress.
  • Campaigns you join: the name you chose to show, your choice of whether the Warden may change your sheet, and a short summary of your character (name, Hit Protection, and conditions) that the other players can see.
  • Play: the shared activity feed (rolls and sheet changes), requests between the Warden and players, and whether you have the campaign open right now.

If you pay for a plan

Payments are taken by our payment provider. We receive your plan, its renewal date and a customer identifier. We never receive or store your card number.

If you use the AI helpers

When you press a button that asks the AI for help, the text needed to answer (for example, part of your campaign brief) is sent to an AI model through our AI provider, and the answer comes back. We keep a count of how many requests you have made, to apply your plan's allowance. We ask our provider not to use this text to train models.

Technical records

Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs, including IP addresses, for security and to find faults. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who can see what

  • Your character sheet is seen by you and by the Warden of the campaign it is in. Other players see only the summary.
  • A Warden's private notes, planned cards and creatures are seen only by that Warden.
  • The brief, revealed cards, the public timeline and the activity feed are seen by everyone in the campaign.
  • Anyone with a campaign's join code can join it. Wardens can remove members.

Who else handles your information

We use these companies to run the app. They handle information on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase): hosting, sign-in, and the database. Information is stored in the United States.
  • Our payment provider (to be named before paid plans open): payments.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to: the AI helpers, once they open.

We share information with no one else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your characters, your memberships and the campaigns you run are deleted within 30 days. Lines you added to another Warden's activity feed remain, with your display name. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

Your choices

  • See or download what we hold: Account, then “Export my data”.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app.
  • Delete it: Account, then “Delete my account”.
  • Ask us anything, or object to how we use your information: {{CONTACT}}. We answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Younger players can take part as a guest, which asks for no personal details, or by having the Warden run their character for them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, write to {{CONTACT}} and we will delete it.

What is kept on your device

The app keeps your sign-in, a copy of your characters and campaigns so that it works offline, and a character you are part-way through making. These are needed for the app to work. We set no advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
