The Waymark app

A rulebook that comes with its own table

Most games hand you a book and wish you luck. Waymark comes with a free app made for these rules, so the bookkeeping takes care of itself and the evening goes to the story.

  • Characters in ten minutes. Six guided steps. Tap to roll, or type in what your own dice showed.
  • Sheets that do the sums. Damage, Armor, Fatigue, Scars and rests, worked out by the rules. They work without a signal.
  • A table that stays in step. Players join with a six-letter code. No account needed.
  • The whole campaign in one place. A brief, a timeline, cards for people, places, fights and loot, and a fight tracker.
  • Your own world. Make Origins, Abilities, gear and creatures, with the rulebook's advice as you go.
  • Help when you want it. Ask for a creature, a place or a recap, and check it before anything is kept.

What it sounds like

The game happens in conversation

Warden The bridge ahead is rope and old planks. It sways in the wind. On the far side, a lantern is moving between the trees.

Sam Do the ropes look like they'd hold us?

Warden The ropes look sound. A few planks in the middle are gray and split.

Sam I'll cross slowly and test each plank before I put weight on it.

One person describes the world. Everyone else decides what to do in it. The rules step in only when nobody knows what should happen next.

Getting started

Your first game, in four steps

  1. Gather two to five people. One of you will be the Warden, who describes the world. The rest each play one character.
  2. Read the Introduction. It explains the dice, the three attributes, and the one roll the game is built on.
  3. Make characters together. Part Two walks you through it step by step, and so does the app.
  4. Play. The Warden describes a place and a problem. Everyone else decides what to do about it.
