Using the app

The Waymark app keeps character sheets and campaigns, rolls dice (or takes the result from your real ones), and can help a Warden prepare. This page is a short guide. The app is a preview and will change.

For players

Getting in

Open the app. Choose Continue as a guest to start at once with no account, or Continue with Google to keep your character on any device. A guest can become an account later and keep everything.

Making a character

Choose Make a character and follow the six steps. At each roll you can tap the dice button, or roll real dice and type what they showed. If you stop part-way, your choices are kept.

Your sheet

  • Take damage works out Armor, HP and Strength for you, and asks for a Scar or a Strength save when the rules call for one.
  • Rest offers a few minutes' rest, a night somewhere safe, a week with care, and a night at a haven.
  • Use beside an Ability adds the Fatigue and asks for a Willpower save if you are in danger.
  • Each item can be in hand, worn or in pack. Two hands hold two things.
  • Undo puts back the last change.

The sheet works without a connection and catches up when you are back online.

Joining a campaign

Your Warden gives you a six-letter code, a link, or a picture to scan. Choose Join with a code, then bring your character in. You choose how the Warden may change your sheet: ask me first, where each change waits for you to accept it, or directly. You can change your mind at any time.

Other players see only your character's name, HP and condition. The Warden sees the whole sheet.

For Wardens

Starting

Choose Start a campaign. Share the code with your players. For a player with no device, choose Add a character you run; you can hand that character over to them later with a one-time code.

The party

Act on a character to deal damage, heal, add Fatigue, give or take an item, award a Tier, or call for a save. For a Blast, tick several characters and roll for each. Everything you do appears in the feed for the table.

Story

  • The brief is the setting and each session's mission. Players can read it.
  • The timeline takes one sentence at a time. Mark the moments that matter as milestones. Tick Only I can see this for a private note.
  • Cards are for people, places, creatures, factions, items and open threads. A planned card is yours alone until you reveal it, and players never see your private notes.

Fight

Start a fight, add enemies, and tick who made their Dexterity save. The tracker keeps the turn order, takes damage off enemies through their Armor, and tells you when a Strength save or a morale check is due.

Workshop

Make your own creatures, Origins, Abilities and gear from the rulebook's forms. The app shows the rulebook's advice as you type and still lets you decide. Origins and Abilities you make appear when anyone builds a character for that campaign. Save the lot as a world to use again.

AI help

Buttons marked Suggest ask an AI for one idea: a creature, a person, a place, an item, an Origin or an Ability. It fills in the ordinary form. Nothing is saved until you have looked at it and saved it yourself. On the Story tab it can also write a recap of the session and propose cards from the timeline. Ask the rulebook on the home screen answers a rules question and links to the rule.

The AI Warden

With the AI Warden plan, a campaign's owner can let an AI run it. It describes the world and reads its narration aloud. The app's rules decide every roll and every number, and players roll their own saves. The owner can read and correct everything it records, and take the campaign back at any time.

Plans

The free plan holds one character of your own and one campaign you run. Joining campaigns is never limited. See the Account page in the app for the paid plan. If a paid plan ends, nothing is deleted: what is over the limit can be read but not changed until you choose what to keep.

Your data

From the Account page you can download everything the app holds about you, or delete your account. See the privacy policy and terms, both still drafts.
