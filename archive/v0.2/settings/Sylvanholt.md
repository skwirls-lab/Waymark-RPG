# SYLVANHOLT: THE FIRST SONG

**A world for Waymark.** This file supplies the names, lists, and creatures. The rules are in the Waymark Rulebook.

Before there was green, there was gray. Sylvanholt—the massive, forest-dominated primary continent of the world of Aelinra—was born from silence when the Golden Stag breathed the First Song into its primordial soil. Through this living resonance, the First Oak sprouted as a monumental biotechnological amplifier, anchoring the Song into the deep roots of the earth. From its golden acorns, six distinct anthropomorphic tribes were seeded to tend and live in harmony with the continent: the industrious Skwirls of the high canopy, the scholarly Mycen burrowers, the grounded Veylin seers, the sleek Shryg scouts, the massive Bavar builders, and the cunning Ratkyn survivors.

Magic in Sylvanholt is not an abstract arcane formula; it is the practical tuning of personal biological frequencies with the First Song. Those who align with its creative, golden resonance channel Ardenfire—manifesting as Hearthnotes (healing), Shieldnotes (warding), Blazenotes (heat/flame), Rootnotes (organic masonry), or Wildnotes (fauna communion). However, this harmony is under siege. Noctis, an ancient figure of pride and ambition, twisted the sacred relics left by the Stag to forge Noctimancy—a sickly violet dissonance that corrupts, decays, and controls.

Your campaign begins in a pivotal, gritty era: one to two generations after the Golden Stag departed Aelinra and the catastrophic trauma of the Great Root Severing weakened the ambient Song. The world is not yet ancient ruins; it is actively fighting for its future. The Great Holds—such as the river-fortress of Timberdeep—are currently being engineered and built. As adventurers, you are not mere fortune hunters looting forgotten tombs. You are partisans, scouts, and defenders holding the line against corrupted forces of Noctis and his top general, Malakar, destroying Riftbound Anchors, attuning historical gear at Resonant Anvils, and protecting living communities before the Song fades into silence.

## The five Harmonies

#### 1. Hearthnotes (Healing & Purification)
* **Soothing Hum:** Restores **1d6 STR** to a touched living creature.
* **Mend the Severed:** Knits simple physical splits, cracked wood, or snapped handles back into structural integrity.
* **Purifying Breath:** Exhales a fragrant vapor clearing non-Noctimantic poisons, mundane infections, or blindness from a target within reach.
* **Restorative Warmth:** Envelops a resting party in golden light; allows clearing **2 Fatigue** slots instead of 1 during a safe night's rest.

#### 2. Shieldnotes (Warding & Density)
* **Hardened Grain:** Aligns an object's density. Target behaves as twice as thick and twice as heavy against physical blows for 10 minutes.
* **Radiant Dome:** Drives staff/paws into floor. A shimmering dome of amber light extends in a 5ft radius, granting **+2 Armor** to allies inside against external physical projectiles for 3 rounds.
* **Resonant Ward:** Traces a golden circle. Selected creature type cannot cross the boundary unless passing a WIL save.
* **Anchor the Pylon:** Infuses a building or bridge with an anchor, rendering it immune to structural collapse from external pressure for 1 hour.

#### 3. Blazenotes (Kinetic Heat & Flare)
* **Blaze Flare:** Fires a concentrated line of heat dealing **1d8 damage** to a target within 30ft. *(Azure variant deals 1d12 and ignores Noctimantic Armor).*
* **Searing Crescent:** Releasing a horizontal arc of golden flame across a 15ft width, dealing **1d6 damage to all targets** in the zone.
* **Ember Sentinel:** Floating orb of gold light hovers above shoulder. Illuminates within 50ft; can detonate on command for **1d6 Blast damage** within 10ft.
* **Ignite Sap:** Boils sap in a wooden weapon/tool within sight. Deals **1d6 ongoing damage** per round to anyone holding it until dropped or extinguished.

#### 4. Rootnotes (Organic Masonry)
* **Thorn Whip:** Thorn-studded vine erupts from soil/bark, lashing a target within 20ft for **1d6 damage** and binding limbs on a failed DEX save.
* **Listening Grove:** Pressing palm to a living tree trunk allows hearing any movement or whisper traveling through root networks within 500ft for 1 minute.
* **Brambleshield:** Wall of dense briers surges from earth (10ft long, 5ft high). Provides cover and inflicts **1d6 damage** to anyone forcing through it.
* **Unfurl the Scroll:** Nearby roots and branches move slowly to form ladders, stairs, or bridges across gaps up to 20ft wide.

#### 5. Wildnotes (Animal Alignment)
* **Beast-Tongue:** Speak with and understand mundane forest fauna (birds, beetles, small critters) for 10 minutes.
* **Soothe the Pack:** Waves of calming amber light wash over aggressive, non-corrupted beasts. They will not attack unless provoked directly.
* **Thornrunner's Accord:** Touching a wild animal or free Thornrunner while passing a WIL save causes it to assist in a single task (tracking, bearing loads, navigating hazards) before departing.
* **Mote Swarm:** Summon specialized fireflies that pulse in unison with your heartbeat to blind opponents, illuminate caverns, or reveal hidden tripwires.

## Noctimantic contamination

Noctimancy (Grave, Rift, Blight, Wither, Veil) distorts and consumes the Song.

### 1. Blight-Rot (Chronic Contamination)
* **Trigger:** Suffer critical damage (STR loss) from a Blightbound creature or corrupted weapon.
* **Effect:** Marked with *Blight-Rot*. At the start of each in-game day, make a **WIL save**.
* **Risk:** Each failure spreads shadow along your veins. Accumulating **3 failures** before finding a high-resonance Sanctuary or receiving treatment from a Mycen Hearthnote **permanently burns away 1 Tier of Attunement Depth**.

### 2. Rift-Discord (Acute Contamination)
* **Trigger:** Proximity to active Riftbound Anchors, Dissonance explosions, or specific enemy abilities.
* **Effect:** While afflicted, all **WIL saves for Harmonic Abilities are made with Disadvantage**.
* **Risk:** Misfires trigger catastrophic backfires. Channeling while under Discord generates **Dissonant Fatigue** tokens that lock inventory slots and cannot be cleared by standard rests (must be purged at a Sanctuary).

### 3. Tier 0 Corruption (The Final Fall)
* **Trigger:** If Blight-Rot burns a character's Attunement Depth down to **Tier 0**.
* **Consequence:** The character's spirit and biology completely succumb to the shadow. They are permanently transformed into a corrupted **Unsung NPC** under Warden control. The character is effectively "dead" to the party, becoming an enemy threat unless a specific narrative arc is undertaken to save them.

**Places of safety** are **Sanctuaries**: ancient groves and other high-resonance sites.

## Resonant Anvils

Gear taken from ordinary battles is **Unsung**. At a Resonant Anvil left by the Stag, it can be tuned.

- **Catalyst to attune:** one chunk of Songstone or Amber Sap Crystal.
- **Catalyst to corrupt:** one shard of Violet Ore.

* **Attuned Weapon (Ardenfire):** Gains *Resonant* quality. Damage is enhanced (**1d12**) when standing in Sanctuaries or high-resonance groves. Flashes with warm gold light upon impact.
* **Attuned Armor/Shield:** Grants **+1 to WIL saves** against mental illusions, fear, and Noctimantic delirium while worn.
* **Corrupted Weapon (Noctimancy):** Critical damage direct to STR automatically inflicts **Blight-Rot** on the target.
* **Corrupted Armor/Shield:** Target's armor is reduced by 1 against incoming Blazenotes/Ardenfire; grants absolute immunity to cold/Grave chills.

## Running Sylvanholt

- **The partisan loop.** Build sessions around tactical objectives: purging Riftbound Anchors, protecting living settlements, attuning gear at Resonant Anvils, and escorting civilians from contested sectors.
- **Voice.** Use the oaths of the world: *"Stag's mercy," "By the Roots," "The Song endures," "Dissonant filth."*

## Bestiary

### **Root Goblin**
* **Stats:** 4 HP, 0 Armor, 8 STR, 14 DEX, 8 WIL, Spear (d6)
* *Descriptor:* Small, wiry thieves with moss-colored fur and twitching ears.
* *Quirk:* Avoid open fights unless numerical advantage is massive. Prize Resonant Slabs and will trade secrets for them.
* *Critical Damage:* Spear tip breaks off in wound; target cannot recover DEX or run cleanly until extracted by a healer.
### **Hooded Men (Watchers of the Wood)**
* **Stats:** 12 HP, 1 Armor, 9 STR, 12 DEX, 14 WIL, Leystaff (d8), **Harmonic Ability (Dread Whisper or Fracture)**
* *Descriptor:* Elongated, robed cultists whose faces are obscured by veils of woven shadow. Power derived from corrupted root-lines.
* *Quirk:* Speak in rhythmic harmonies that cause nearby firefly lanterns to flicker out of sync.
* *Critical Damage:* Leech spiritual focus, dealing **1d4 WIL damage** directly.
### **Deadfang**
* **Stats:** 10 HP, 2 Armor, 14 STR, 12 DEX, 6 WIL, Serrated Bite (d10)
* *Descriptor:* Massive, malformed hunting hound reconstructed through advanced Noctimancy. Bone plates fused to skin, veins pulsing purple.
* *Quirk:* *Resonance Tracker.* Ignores visual stealth. Moves at double speed toward any character who has cast Ardenfire within the last hour.
* *Critical Damage:* Serrated bite shatters weapons. Non-attuned weapons used to parry/strike the Deadfang break on a critical failure.
### **Wood Troll**
* **Stats:** 12 HP, 0 Armor, 15 STR, 12 DEX, 7 WIL, Claws & Bite (d8+d8 Blast)
* *Descriptor:* Towering, moss-covered monstrosity with rotting bark flesh and emerald sap blood.
* *Quirk:* *Rapid Re-knitting.* Can take an action to instantly recover all lost HP.
* *Critical Damage:* Fungal moss and twigs grow out of target's wounds, rendering them **Deprived** until burned clear.
