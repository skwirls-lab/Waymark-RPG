# Waymark — decisions log

Rulings made to get draft 0.1 written, and questions still open. Nothing here is playtested.

## 1. Answers to the nine open questions in the Disposition Matrix

| # | Question | Ruling in draft 0.1 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Origin vs Lineage vs Background | **Origin** = what you are (ancestry, culture, manufacture). **Background** = what you did (Cairn's career list). Settings may rename Origin (Sylvanholt: Tribe). | High |
| 2 | Can Origins grant numbers? | Yes, within a budget: one situational Trait, at most one Edge (+1 ability, or +2 slots, or Advantage vs a narrow threat), a Burden if strong. Never HP, Armor, damage, actions. | Medium |
| 3 | Fatigue or broader cost? | Broader. Fatigue is the default of eight cost profiles (module 3). | Medium |
| 4 | Simplest advancement that fits Attunement Depth | Ranks 1–5, milestones equal to the next Rank's number, 2 Powers known per Rank. Matches Sylvanholt's 2/4/6 caps. | Medium |
| 5 | Which Sylvanholt scar changes are intentional? | Treated all as intentional and kept in the setting pack. See section 3 for the list to confirm. | Needs John |
| 6 | Full inventory, Fatigue, Bavar slots | Full = 0 HP and no HP recovery until a slot frees. No Scar roll from this. Forced Fatigue with no free slot means drop an item. Bavar get 12 slots (8 pack). | Medium |
| 7 | Is corruption reversible? | Each setting decides, but the end state must be known in advance, take 3+ failures, and have a route back through the fiction. | Medium |
| 8 | Movement conventions | Distance bands + four scales: Mind, Skirmish 6" (default for minis), Classic 8", Grid. Move + action, or move twice. Far = three moves. 1" = 5 ft. | Medium |
| 9 | Essential templates | Origin, equipment, Power, enemy, hazard, track, setting pack. | High |

## 2. New rules invented for this draft

All marked [PROPOSED] in the text.

- Advantage / Disadvantage (Sylvanholt used the terms without defining them).
- Maneuvers (target saves, no damage).
- Exploration timing, light durations, travel stages, the d6 event table.
- Generic misfire table.
- Power scope tiers (Minor / Standard / Major) and the 4-Power cap without advancement.
- Rank-up rewards (HP reroll per Rank, d20-over ability bumps).
- Holdings (Defense / Supply / Spirit).
- Heroic scale rule (higher scale enhanced, lower impaired).
- All of module 10 is a sketch.

## 4. Licensing: verify before publishing

- Cairn is CC BY-SA 4.0. **Share-alike means this rulebook's text must also be released under CC BY-SA 4.0.** Anyone may then reuse it, including commercially. This does not extend to a separate companion app's code, but should be confirmed for any rules text embedded in the app.
- The local Cairn reference is a condensed summary, not the official text. The tables reproduced from it (names, traits, gear, spells, bestiary) should be checked against the official SRD at cairnrpg.com before release.
- The local reference appears to be Cairn **1e**. Some procedures in this draft (move-plus-action, three watches per day) resemble Cairn 2e. Confirm which edition is cited and whether 2e's license terms match.
- Confirm the attribution wording in Appendix G with the Cairn site's guidance for third-party creators.
- "Waymark" was checked with a quick web search only. No game by that name turned up, but do a proper search (itch.io, DriveThruRPG, trademark databases) before committing.
