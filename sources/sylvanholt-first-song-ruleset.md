
# SYLVANHOLT: THE FIRST SONG
## An OSR Biotechnological Fantasy Roleplaying Game (Based on Cairn SRD)

---

# INTRODUCTION: WELCOME TO SYLVANHOLT

Before there was green, there was gray. Sylvanholt—the massive, forest-dominated primary continent of the world of Aelinra—was born from silence when the Golden Stag breathed the First Song into its primordial soil. Through this living resonance, the First Oak sprouted as a monumental biotechnological amplifier, anchoring the Song into the deep roots of the earth. From its golden acorns, six distinct anthropomorphic tribes were seeded to tend and live in harmony with the continent: the industrious Skwirls of the high canopy, the scholarly Mycen burrowers, the grounded Veylin seers, the sleek Shryg scouts, the massive Bavar builders, and the cunning Ratkyn survivors.

Magic in Sylvanholt is not an abstract arcane formula; it is the practical tuning of personal biological frequencies with the First Song. Those who align with its creative, golden resonance channel Ardenfire—manifesting as Hearthnotes (healing), Shieldnotes (warding), Blazenotes (heat/flame), Rootnotes (organic masonry), or Wildnotes (fauna communion). However, this harmony is under siege. Noctis, an ancient figure of pride and ambition, twisted the sacred relics left by the Stag to forge Noctimancy—a sickly violet dissonance that corrupts, decays, and controls.

Your campaign begins in a pivotal, gritty era: one to two generations after the Golden Stag departed Aelinra and the catastrophic trauma of the Great Root Severing weakened the ambient Song. The world is not yet ancient ruins; it is actively fighting for its future. The Great Holds—such as the river-fortress of Timberdeep—are currently being engineered and built. As adventurers, you are not mere fortune hunters looting forgotten tombs. You are partisans, scouts, and defenders holding the line against corrupted forces of Noctis and his top general, Malakar, destroying Riftbound Anchors, attuning historical gear at Resonant Anvils, and protecting living communities before the Song fades into silence.

## CHAPTER 1: CORE PHILOSOPHIES & MECHANICS

### Design Philosophies
* **Classless Advancement:** A character's specialty is defined entirely by their physical gear, their historical attunements, and the Harmonic Abilities they channel.
* **The Vulnerability of Flesh:** Death is an ever-present risk in the deep wilds. Combat is fast, dangerous, and violent. Avoidance, strategy, and negotiation are usually wiser than drawing steel.
* **Fiction First:** Dice do not dictate absolute narrative reality. Outcomes are arbitrated by the Warden through conversational logic, tribal biology, and environmental positioning.
* **Resonant Growth:** Characters grow by discovering ancient groves, purging Riftbound Anchors, surviving encounters with corrupted forces, and attuning gear at Resonant Anvils.

---

### The Three Ability Scores
Roll **3d6** for each score in order, then swap any two. Add +1 for your chosen Tribal Lineage (Max 18).

* **Strength (STR):** Brawn, muscle memory, physical endurance, and structural resistance. Used for lifting heavy timber, resisting physical poisons, and bearing heavy armor.
* **Dexterity (DEX):** Agility, poise, quick reflexes, stealth, and balancing along canopy walkways. Used for moving silently, dodging traps, and determining combat priority.
* **Willpower (WIL):** Mental fortitude, confidence, focus, and spiritual alignment with the First Song. Used for persuading wary factions, resisting Noctimantic delirium, and channeling Harmonic Abilities.

---

### Ability Saves
When a risky or uncertain action occurs, roll a **d20 equal to or under** the appropriate Ability Score.
* **Rolling equal to or under your stat = Success.**
* **Rolling over your stat = Failure.**
* A roll of **1** is always a success; a **20** is always a failure.

---

### Combat & Damage Resolution
Combat prioritizes narrative speed and tactical positioning over grid movements.

#### 1. Turn Priority
The Warden telegraphs enemy intentions clearly. At the start of combat, players make a **DEX save**.
* **Success:** Players act before opponents.
* **Failure:** Opponents execute their telegraphed actions first.

#### 2. Auto-Hit Attacks
Attacks hit automatically; there are no rolls to determine hits or misses.
* The attacker rolls their weapon's damage die, subtracts the target's total **Armor**, and deals the remaining amount directly to the target's **Hit Protection (HP)**.
* **Unarmed Attacks:** Always deal **1d4** damage.
* **Dual Weapons:** Roll both damage dice and retain the **single highest result**.
* **Multiple Attackers:** If multiple foes assault one target, roll all damage dice simultaneously and keep the **single highest result**.

#### 3. Attack Modifiers
* **Impaired:** If bound, blind, operating from severe tactical disadvantage, or using broken tools, roll **1d4** damage total.
* **Enhanced:** If leveraging high ground, ambushing, or striking a helpless foe, roll **1d12** damage total.
* **Blast Quality:** Attacks marked as *Blast* affect all targets in a designated area. Roll damage separately for each target.

#### 4. Armor Rules
* Subtract total Armor value from incoming damage rolls.
* Shields and helmets each grant **+1 Armor** while actively held or worn.
* **Maximum Armor Cap:** **3**.

---

### Health, Scars & Critical Damage
* **Hit Protection (HP):** HP reflects luck, poise, focus, and spiritual gumption to avoid a killing blow. Resting for a few moments with fresh water completely restores lost HP.
* **Exactly 0 HP:** When an attack takes a character's HP exactly to **0**, immediately roll **1d12** on the **Master Scars Table**.
* **Critical Damage:** When an attack deals damage beyond a character's remaining HP, the excess damage reduces their **STR** score directly. The player must immediately make a **STR save**.
    * **Failure:** The character falls unconscious, begins bleeding out, and dies within one hour unless treated by a healer or stabilized with an Ardenfire Hearthnote.
    * **Any STR Reduction:** Immediately roll on the Master Scars Table.

### Inventory, Overburdening & Fatigue
Characters possess a baseline of **10 inventory slots** (6 in backpack, 2 hands, 2 upper body). *Bavar characters possess **12 slots**.*
* **Item Sizes:** Standard gear, tools, and weapons occupy **1 slot**. Small items can be bundled together. **Bulky items** (heavy warhammers, longbows, chainmail) occupy **2 slots**.
* **Overburdened State:** Anyone carrying full inventory slots is immediately reduced to **0 HP** due to physical exhaustion. A character cannot carry more items than their total slot capacity.
* **Harmonic Ability Sheet:** Known Harmonic Abilities **do not occupy physical inventory slots**. They are tracked separately on the sheet.
* **Fatigue:** Channeling a Harmonic Ability, traveling without rations, or suffering specific Noctimantic attacks adds **Fatigue** tokens directly into inventory slots. Each Fatigue occupies **1 slot**, rendering it unusable until cleared by a full night's rest in a safe sanctuary.

## CHAPTER 2: THE SIX TRIBES & CHARACTER GENESIS

### Step-by-Step Character Creation
1. **Attributes:** Roll **3d6** for STR, DEX, and WIL in order. You may swap any two.
2. **Resilience:** Roll **1d6** for starting **Hit Protection (HP)**.
3. **Tribal Lineage:** Choose one of the Six Tribes and apply their attribute bonus (+1, max 18) and anatomical trait.
4. **Harmony Affinity:** Select your Primary Affinity (**Hearth, Shield, Blaze, Root, or Wild**). Non-practitioner backgrounds keep this affinity *latent* until awakened by narrative events.
5. **Starting Logistics:** Begin with 3 days of rations (1 slot), a light source (1 slot), and **3d6 Gold Pieces (gp)**.
6. **Starting Equipment:** Roll a **1d6** on your Tribe's Starting Equipment Matrix.

## CHAPTER 4: THE FIRST SONG & CHARACTER PROGRESSION

### Harmonic Abilities
Harmonic Abilities are manifestations of Ardenfire channeled through a practitioner's (**Note's**) innate alignment with the First Song.
* **Tracking:** Known abilities are written on your sheet and **do not occupy physical inventory slots**.
* **Fatigue Cost:** Channeling an ability instantly adds **1 Fatigue** token directly into an inventory slot.
* **Downtime Amplification:** In positions of safety with time to align breathing, a Note can amplify the scale or duration of an ability without incurring additional Fatigue.
* **Dangerous Channeling:** Channeling while in danger or deprived requires a **WIL save**.
    * **Success:** Ability executes as intended.
    * **Failure:** Misfire. The Warden arbitrates an inverse effect—severe physical damage, an explosion of extra Fatigue slots, or immediate unconsciousness.

---

### Attinement Depth (Character Progression)
Characters do not gain XP or level up via coin hoarding. Progression is measured by **Attunement Depth (Tiers 1 to 5)**.
* **Tier 1 (Latent/Awakened):** Knows 1–2 baseline Harmonic Abilities within Primary Affinity.
* **Tier 2 (Attuned Practitioner):** Knows up to 4 abilities within Primary Affinity.
* **Tier 3 (Master Note):** Knows up to 6 abilities; can begin discovering a **Secondary Affinity**.
* **Tier 4 (Full Chord Resonance):** Can master abilities across both Primary and Secondary Affinities.
* **Tier 5 (Echo of the Stag):** Legendary resonance.

---

### The Five Ardenfire Harmonies

#### 1. Hearthnotes (Healing & Purification)
* **Soothing Hum:** Restores **1d6 STR** to a touched living creature.
* **Mend the Severed:** Knits simple physical splits, cracked wood, or snapped handles back into structural integrity.
* **Purifying Breath:** Exhales a fragrant vapor clearing non-Noctimantic poisons, mundane infections, or blindness from a target within reach.
* **Restorative Warmth:** Envelops a resting party in golden light; allows clearing **2 Fatigue** slots instead of 1 during a safe night's rest.

#### 2. Shieldnotes (Warding & Density)
* **Hardened Grain:** Align's an object's density. Target behaves as twice as thick and twice as heavy against physical blows for 10 minutes.
* **Radiant Dome:** Drives staff/paws into floor. A shimmering dome of amber light extends in a 5ft radius, granting **+2 Armor** to allies inside against external physical projectiles for 3 rounds.
* **Resonant Ward:** Traces a golden circle. Selected creature type cannot cross the boundary unless passing a WIL save.
* **Anchor the Pylon:** Infuses a building or bridge with an anchor, rendering it immune to structural collapse from external pressure for 1 hour.

#### 3. Blazenotes (Kinetic Heat & Flare)
* **Blaze Flare:** Fires a concentrated line of heat dealing **1d8 damage** to a target within 30ft. *(Azure variant deals 1d12 and ignores Noctimantic Armor).*
* **Searing Crescent:** Releasing a horizontal arc of golden flame across a 15ft width, dealing **1d6 damage to all targets** in the zone.
* **Ember Sentinel:** Floating orb of gold light hovers above shoulder. Illuminates within 50ft; can detonate on command for **1d6 Blast damage** within 10ft.
* **Ignite Sap:** Boils sap in a wooden weapon/tool within sight. Deals **1d6 ongoing damage** per round to anyone holding it until dropped or extinguished.

#### 4. Rootnotes (Organic Masonry)
* **Thorn Whip:** Thorn-studded vine erupts from soil/bark, lashing a target within 20ft for **1d6 damage** and binding limbs on a failed DEX save.
* **Listening Grove:** Pressing palm to a living tree trunk allows hearing any movement or whisper traveling through root networks within 500ft for 1 minute.
* **Brambleshield:** Wall of dense briers surges from earth (10ft long, 5ft high). Provides cover and inflicts **1d6 damage** to anyone forcing through it.
* **Unfurl the Scroll:** Nearby roots and branches move slowly to form ladders, stairs, or bridges across gaps up to 20ft wide.

#### 5. Wildnotes (Animal Alignment)
* **Beast-Tongue:** Speak with and understand mundane forest fauna (birds, beetles, small critters) for 10 minutes.
* **Soothe the Pack:** Waves of calming amber light wash over aggressive, non-corrupted beasts. They will not attack unless provoked directly.
* **Thornrunner's Accord:** Touching a wild animal or free Thornrunner while passing a WIL save causes it to assist in a single task (tracking, bearing loads, navigating hazards) before departing.
* **Mote Swarm:** Summon specialized fireflies that pulse in unison with your heartbeat to blind opponents, illuminate caverns, or reveal hidden tripwires.

## CHAPTER 5: NOCTIMANTIC CONTAMINATION & CORRUPTION

Noctimancy (Grave, Rift, Blight, Wither, Veil) distorts and consumes the Song. Players **cannot** select Noctimantic Dissonances at character creation.

### 1. Blight-Rot (Chronic Contamination)
* **Trigger:** Suffer critical damage (STR loss) from a Blightbound creature or corrupted weapon.
* **Effect:** Marked with *Blight-Rot*. At the start of each in-game day, make a **WIL save**.
* **Risk:** Each failure spreads shadow along your veins. Accumulating **3 failures** before finding a high-resonance Sanctuary or receiving treatment from a Mycen Hearthnote **permanently burns away 1 Tier of Attunement Depth**.

### 2. Rift-Discord (Acute Contamination)
* **Trigger:** Proximity to active Riftbound Anchors, Dissonance explosions, or specific enemy abilities.
* **Effect:** While afflicted, all **WIL saves for Harmonic Abilities are made with Disadvantage**.
* **Risk:** Misfires trigger catastrophic backfires. Channeling while under Discord generates **Dissonant Fatigue** tokens that lock inventory slots and cannot be cleared by standard rests (must be purged at a Sanctuary).

### 3. Tier 0 Corruption (The Final Fall)
* **Trigger:** If Blight-Rot burns a character's Attunement Depth down to **Tier 0**.
* **Consequence:** The character's spirit and biology completely succumb to the shadow. They are permanently transformed into a corrupted **Unsung NPC** under Warden control. The character is effectively "dead" to the party, becoming an enemy threat unless a specific narrative arc is undertaken to save them.

## CHAPTER 6: CRAFTING AT RESONANT ANVILS

Items scavenged from standard battles are usually **Unsung** or **Silent**. Players can use ancient **Resonant Anvils** left behind by the Stag to attune or corrupt gear during downtime.

```
   [UNSUNG / SILENT OBJECT]
     /                  \
    /                    \
```

- Songstone + Violet Shard
    
- WIL Save (Pass) + Forced Submission /
    
    v v [ATTUNED / HARMONIZED] [CORRUPTED / DISSONANT] (Warm Gold / Amber) (Oily Violet / Smoke)

### Tuning Procedure 
1. Place 1 Unsung weapon, shield, or tool onto the basin of a Resonant Anvil. 
2. Consume 1 chunk of **Songstone/Amber Sap Crystal** (for Attunement) OR 1 shard of **Violet Ore** (for Corruption). 
3. Primary crafter makes a **WIL save**. 
* **Success:** Item transforms. 
* **Failure:** Frequency fractures. Catalyst is consumed, item remains Unsung, and crafter gains **1 Fatigue**. 
### Item Properties 
* **Attuned Weapon (Ardenfire):** Gains *Resonant* quality. Damage is enhanced (**1d12**) when standing in Sanctuaries or high-resonance groves. Flashes with warm gold light upon impact. 
* **Attuned Armor/Shield:** Grants **+1 to WIL saves** against mental illusions, fear, and Noctimantic delirium while worn. 
* **Corrupted Weapon (Noctimancy):** Critical damage direct to STR automatically inflicts **Blight-Rot** on the target. 
* **Corrupted Armor/Shield:** Target's armor is reduced by 1 against incoming Blazenotes/Ardenfire; grants absolute immunity to cold/Grave chills.

## CHAPTER 7: WARDEN & PLAYER PRINCIPLES 
### Principles for Wardens 
* **Telegraph Danger:** Put hazards in plain sight. Let players solve problems through critical thinking. 
* **Context over Math:** If something is impossible, don't allow a roll. If sensible, let it happen automatically. 
* **The Partisan Loop:** Focus campaigns around tactical objectives: purging Riftbound Anchors, protecting living settlements, attuning gear at Resonant Anvils, and escorting civilians from contested sectors. 
* **Vernacular Authenticity:** Use world [[oaths]] (*"Stag's mercy," "By the Roots," "The Song endures," "Dissonant filth"*). 
### Principles for Players 
* **Agency over Stats:** Stats are tools, not limits. Ask what you would do in the situation. 
* **Caution over Violence:** Combat is brutal and auto-hits. Stack the odds in your favor, ambush, or negotiate. 
* **Exploration:** Ask detailed questions about the environment. Listening to details is more useful than any stat.
