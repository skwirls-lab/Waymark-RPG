# Cairn Multiverse Rule Disposition Matrix

**Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Purpose:** Convert the [[Cairn-Multiverse-Rules-Audit]] into working design dispositions for a complete, self-contained rulebook. The final book will be fully rewritten and exhaustive: it must contain all rules needed to play without requiring readers to consult the Cairn reference or the Sylvanholt rules document. Those source documents remain research and provenance references during development. This matrix records direction and proposed treatment; it does not itself constitute the complete rulebook or claim every row is a final/playtested rule.

## Disposition key

- **CORE — retain:** Keep in the baseline Cairn-compatible game. Preserve original procedure unless clearly labeled otherwise.
- **OPTIONAL — generalize:** Develop as a setting-neutral module that a campaign can include or omit.
- **SETTING — Sylvanholt:** Keep the mechanic’s current implementation and fiction in the Sylvanholt setting pack.
- **DECIDE — verify:** The source comparison shows an omission, ambiguity, or design conflict that needs an explicit ruling before drafting.
- **OUT — exclude:** Deliberately do not include in the shared system. No rules are assigned OUT yet; absence in either source is not evidence of intentional exclusion.

## C. Powers, advancement, and extraordinary abilities

| Rule / source | Proposed disposition | Working ruling and reason |
|---|---|---|
| Cairn spellbooks, scrolls, relics, WIL casting risk, Fatigue | COMPATIBILITY OPTION — keep available, not the preferred expansion model | Retain the Cairn magic engine as a reference/compatibility option for groups that want it, but do not make spellbooks the default template for the universal Powers module. John’s design preference is to generalize the Sylvanholt approach: defined abilities with explicit use costs and risk under pressure, translated into each setting’s fiction. |
| Sylvanholt Harmonic Abilities and five Ardenfire affinities | SETTING — Sylvanholt; OPTIONAL — generalize | Keep Ardenfire and the ability lists in Sylvanholt. Build an optional Powers module with acquisition, effect scope, cost, limits, risk, and recovery. The module should support magic, technology, mutation, training, or other fictional sources without asserting they are identical in-world. |
| Fatigue costs for powers; WIL save and misfire under danger/deprivation | OPTIONAL — generalize; SETTING — Sylvanholt | Prototype as one possible power-cost/risk profile, not a universal cost. Define whether costs are paid on failure, what safe use means, misfire bounds, stacking, and clearing. Keep specific misfires in Sylvanholt. |
| Cairn’s emergent/in-world growth | CORE — retain | Keep as the default advancement philosophy; do not require levels or tiers. |
| Sylvanholt Attunement Depth (Tiers 1–5 and ability caps) | SETTING — Sylvanholt; OPTIONAL — generalize | Keep the existing tier track as Sylvanholt’s implementation. Offer Structured Advancement as an optional module for campaigns that want milestones or ranks; do not make it the universal growth model. |
| Power acquisition / training / discoveries | OPTIONAL — generalize | Define portable ways to acquire or change extraordinary capabilities while leaving methods to setting fiction. Avoid granting new powers solely by level unless a selected advancement module says so. |

## G. Decisions fixed by current direction vs. still open

### Direction established by John

- Treat the Cairn and Sylvanholt documents in the shared vault as the authoritative current versions.
- **Complete standalone book:** The intended deliverable is a fully rewritten, exhaustive rulebook with a complete default Core Rules path, self-contained optional/expanded modules, setting packs, and make-your-own guidance. End users must not need the legacy Cairn or Sylvanholt rules documents to play. See [[Cairn-Multiverse-Standalone-Rulebook-Blueprint]]. The v0.1 prototype is only a partial component, not a full ruleset.
- Generalize Sylvanholt-derived concepts and make them optional where appropriate, to support different settings and character types.
- Keep setting-specific Sylvanholt content distinct from the portable rules.
- Use this matrix as the next design step after the audit.
- Generalize Sylvanholt’s defined-ability + explicit-cost + risk-under-pressure pattern as the preferred Powers design model; do not treat Cairn spellbooks as the default template (they may remain a separate compatibility option).
- Make movement distance/tabletop scale configurable so groups can choose a convention that fits their table and miniatures, rather than enforcing one inches-per-turn value.
- Provide guided customization templates for player-created Origins, equipment, spells/Powers, and other modular content.
- Longer-term, publish the rules and guidance on a website/wiki; explore a character/campaign/Warden companion app with free and paid features. Map-building software is deferred while Arkenforge meets the need.

### Provisional dispositions in this matrix

These are recommended defaults for the project, not individually playtested rules text: keep the Cairn engine as baseline; retain Cairn’s omitted procedures unless deliberately ruled out; make Origins, Powers, Structured Advancement, Corruption/Stress, and Item Modification optional modules; keep Sylvanholt’s implementation and lore in its setting pack.

### Explicit design questions before a playable draft

1. Should the universal expansion use “Origin,” “Lineage,” “Background,” or distinct terms for biological ancestry versus social/career identity?
2. Should generic origins be purely fictional permissions, or may they grant ability/inventory changes? What balance ceiling is acceptable?
3. Powers use the Sylvanholt defined-ability/cost/risk pattern as the preferred optional model; spellbooks remain a separate compatibility option. Remaining question: should generic Powers track Fatigue specifically, or use a broader cost profile with Fatigue as one choice?
4. What is the simplest complete advancement option that preserves Cairn’s default while representing Attunement Depth for Sylvanholt?
5. Which Sylvanholt scar outcomes intentionally replace Cairn outcomes, and which are edits that should be reconciled?
6. In Sylvanholt, does being at full inventory immediately set HP to 0, and how do Fatigue tokens, Bavar capacity, and the hands/upper-body slots interact?
7. Is corruption meant to be reversible in all modules, or does each setting decide its endpoint?
8. What tabletop movement conventions should the first scale options support, and what exact source/edition procedures are we comparing?
9. Which customization templates are essential for v0.1 beyond Origins, equipment, and Powers?

## Next steps

2. Audit the exact Cairn movement procedure/edition and Sylvanholt table rule; then design selectable scale options and test table-footprint consequences.
3. Refine the guided Origin, equipment, and Power templates with worked examples and test them across contrasting concepts.
4. Record playtest findings before deciding whether Fatigue should remain the generic cost or become one option in a broader cost framework.
5. Keep advancement, corruption, and item modification for later modules unless the first tests show they are essential.
