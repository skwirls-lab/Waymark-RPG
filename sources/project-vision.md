# Cairn Multiverse Project Vision

**Captured:** 2026-11-23  
**Status:** Early product vision. Confirmed goals are separated from exploratory product ideas.  
**Ruleset destination:** This project is intended to produce a complete, self-contained, rewritten rulebook—not only an expansion document that requires readers to consult Cairn or the current Sylvanholt rules. The final book should include all rules necessary to play, identify **Core Rules** versus **Optional/Expanded Modules** clearly, and integrate a setting-neutral customization guide. Cairn and Sylvanholt source documents remain research/heritage references during development, not required companion rulebooks for end users.

## Confirmed design direction

John likes Cairn’s simplicity and wants to adapt it for more scenarios without losing the recognizable Cairn foundation. The current goal is a modular, Cairn-based rules resource: preserve a clear core, make modifications and expansions selectable, and support different settings and character concepts without turning the system into a dense universal rules engine.

John’s Sylvanholt rules in Obsidian are to be treated as the authoritative final/current Sylvanholt version for this project. Ardenfire abilities and Fatigue were deliberate alternatives to Cairn’s spellbook-style magic. Generalized Powers should therefore draw primarily from the Sylvanholt pattern—defined abilities, explicit costs, and meaningful risk under pressure—rather than treating spellbooks as the default. Cairn spellbooks may remain a separate compatibility option.

### Flexibility and customization

- **Movement and tabletop scale should be configurable.** John has already adjusted movement in his Cairn-derived play and wants groups to choose a tabletop scale that fits their table, minis, and style—for example a shorter movement convention around 5–6 inches or the larger 8-inch/40-foot convention he associates with Cairn. Do not force one tabletop measurement on all campaigns. Audit the exact movement rule against the chosen Cairn source/edition before codifying it; the current local Cairn reference may not include the full movement procedure.
- Core rules and optional modules should be modular where practical.
- Provide structured, easy-to-use guidance/templates so a player can create or customize equipment, Powers/spells, Origins, and eventually other rule content. Guidance should state the fields to define, useful prompts, limits, balance considerations, and examples; it should help people who have ideas but are unsure how to make them playable.
- Custom creation should preserve the lightness of the system: give people enough scaffolding to make coherent, bounded content without requiring point-buy accounting or extensive exception rules.

## Intended public resource

John wants to publish the rules and supporting guidance on his own website or knowledge-base/wiki resource for other people to use.

### Complete, self-contained rulebook requirement

The deliverable is a **fully rewritten, exhaustive, standalone rulebook**. It must contain every procedure needed to create characters, run play, and adjudicate the chosen core and optional modules; a reader should not need the original Cairn reference or the existing Sylvanholt rules document at the table. Those sources remain internal references during design and provenance/licensing research, not external dependencies of the final play experience.

The rulebook should explicitly label and organize:

- **Core Rules:** the default, complete game that works on its own.
- **Optional / Expanded Rules:** complete modules that can be enabled without making the core incomplete; include prerequisites, interactions, and what changes when enabled.
- **Setting Packs:** e.g. Sylvanholt, containing setting-specific fiction, character options, gear, powers, threats, and procedures.
- **Make-Your-Own guidance:** templates and practical steps to create Origins, equipment, powers/spells, and other content, with boundaries and worked examples.
- **Reference material:** concise tables, glossary, character record guidance, Warden tools, examples, and any compatibility/conversion material needed for standalone use.

The book may be developed incrementally—writing a full outline and drafting sections as they are audited—but the prototype must not be mistaken for the finished ruleset. Maintain a single evolving master rulebook (or clearly versioned chapters) and a source/disposition trail so decisions are visible and content is not lost.

The material should distinguish clearly between:

1. the Cairn-derived baseline and attribution;
2. the project’s original modifications and optional modules;
3. setting packs such as Sylvanholt; and
4. examples and player-facing customization templates.

John has already reviewed Cairn attribution and believes publication is permissible with attribution. Before release, independently verify the exact source edition, license text, attribution format, and share-alike requirements for the material actually reused or adapted. Do not present an unverified legal conclusion as settled.

## Companion app concept — exploratory, not scoped

John is interested in a companion app, potentially with free and paid feature tiers. Possible functions include:

- character sheet storage and in-session character sheet use;
- individual campaign tracking;
- Warden/GM tools for managing campaigns;
- guided generation of campaign content;
- optional AI features to help Wardens/players prepare and run games.

This is a future product direction, not an approved feature spec, pricing plan, or implementation commitment. App scope should follow a stable, well-structured rules reference so app mechanics and user content can be tied to versioned modules.

## Separate map-building app — deferred exploration

A second, map-focused application is a possible later idea, but John wants to defer it because Arkenforge already serves that need well. Do not include map software in the first companion-app scope unless John revisits the decision.

## Design principles to carry forward

- **Simple first:** prefer a short procedure and strong examples over a complex universal simulation.
- **Modular by intent:** say what a campaign uses, what it leaves out, and what changes when a module is enabled.
- **Customizable with guidance:** provide templates and authoring advice, not only finished lists.
- **Fiction informs mechanics:** Origins, Powers, gear, and movement should reflect what is happening in the world.
- **Don't homogenize genres:** a superheroic option may need different assumptions from a fragile Cairn-style explorer; identify that boundary rather than quietly changing the core.
- **Separate rules, settings, publishing, and software:** keep the core toolkit, Sylvanholt pack, public documentation, and app roadmap linked but independently understandable.
- **Prototype before broad implementation:** test each module across dissimilar character types and record edge cases before turning it into canonical text or app behavior.

## Next steps

1. Use [[Cairn-Multiverse-Standalone-Rulebook-Blueprint]] as the governing structure for the complete, self-contained rulebook; keep the prototype as a partial design component, not the final deliverable.
2. Build a complete source/disposition ledger and verify exact source/version/provenance for all candidate rules, including movement and tabletop scale.
3. Decide and draft the complete Core Rules first, in original self-contained prose, with all first-session procedures included.
4. Define optional module interfaces and clear compatibility rules; develop the movement-scale options and customization templates as part of that full book.
5. Add and playtest the Sylvanholt setting pack, then address publication, licensing, website/wiki, and eventual companion-app requirements.
